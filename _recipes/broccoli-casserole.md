title: Broccoli Casserole with Ritz Crackers
summary: >-
  Tender broccoli in melted Velveeta, topped with sharp cheddar and a crunchy
  buttered Ritz cracker crust. Even picky eaters ask for seconds.
category: Side
tags: [Make Ahead, Vegetarian]
added: 2026-10-24

# image_alt: Cheesy broccoli casserole with a cracker crumb topping
time:
  prep: 15
  cook: 30
servings: 10
calories: 200
source:
  name: Love From The Oven
  author: Christi Johnstone
  url: https://www.lovefromtheoven.com/broccoli-casserole-with-ritz-crackers/

ingredients:
  - group: Casserole
    items:
      - qty: "32 ounces"
        item: frozen broccoli florets
      - qty: "1 teaspoon"
        item: salt
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: pepper
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: garlic powder
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: onion powder
      - qty: "16 ounces"
        item: Velveeta, cut into cubes no larger than 1 inch
        shop: Velveeta
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: sharp cheddar cheese, grated
  - group: Topping
    items:
      - qty: "1 sleeve"
        item: Ritz crackers, crushed
      - qty: "2 tablespoons"
        item: salted butter, melted

steps:
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: >-
      Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Spray a **9x13-inch** baking dish with
      nonstick spray.
  - title: Blanch the broccoli
    text: >-
      Boil the broccoli in salted water for **3 minutes**, drain, and put it in a
      large bowl.
  - title: Season and add the Velveeta
    text: >-
      Season the broccoli with the salt, pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder.
      Stir in the Velveeta until it's completely melted and mixed through.
  - title: Fill the dish
    text: >-
      Spread the broccoli mixture evenly in the dish and sprinkle the cheddar over
      the top.
  - title: Top and bake
    text: >-
      Toss the crushed crackers with the melted butter and scatter them over the
      top. Bake **25–30 minutes**, until bubbly. Serve right away.

notes:
  - Keeps 5 days in the fridge or up to 3 months in the freezer.
  - Reheat in the air fryer at 350°F for 5–7 minutes so the crackers crisp back up.
