---
title: Old-Fashioned Molasses Cookies
summary: >-
  Soft, spiced molasses cookies rolled in sugar, the kind that taste like
  grandma's gingerbread.
category: Dessert
# image: /assets/images/recipes/old-fashioned-molasses-cookies.jpg
# image_alt: Sugar-crusted molasses cookies on a cooling rack
time:
  prep: 45
  cook: 20
  additional: 60
servings: 36
yield: 3 dozen cookies
source:
  name: Land O'Lakes
  author: Land O'Lakes Test Kitchen
  url: https://www.landolakes.com/recipe/8705/old-fashioned-molasses-cookies/

ingredients:
  - group: Dough
    items:
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: salted butter, softened
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: firmly packed brown sugar
      - qty: "¼ cup"
        item: shortening
      - qty: "¾ cup"
        item: molasses
      - qty: "1 large"
        item: egg
      - qty: "3 cups"
        item: all-purpose flour
      - qty: "1 teaspoon"
        item: baking soda
      - qty: "1 teaspoon"
        item: ground cinnamon
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: ground ginger
      - qty: "¼ teaspoon"
        item: ground cloves
      - qty: "¼ teaspoon"
        item: salt
  - group: For rolling
    items:
      - qty: ""
        item: granulated sugar

steps:
  - title: Cream the wet ingredients
    text: >-
      In a large bowl, beat the butter, brown sugar, shortening, molasses, and egg
      at **medium speed** until creamy, scraping the bowl often.
  - title: Add the dry ingredients
    text: >-
      Turn the mixer to **low** and add the flour, baking soda, cinnamon, ginger,
      cloves, and salt. Mix just until combined.
  - title: Chill the dough
    text: >-
      Divide the dough in half, wrap each piece, and refrigerate for
      **1–2 hours**, until firm enough to roll.
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: Preheat the oven to **375°F**.
  - title: Shape
    text: >-
      Roll the dough into **1½-inch** balls, then roll each in granulated sugar.
      Set them **2 inches** apart on ungreased baking sheets and flatten each to
      about **2½ inches** with the bottom of a glass.
  - title: Bake
    text: >-
      Bake for **8–11 minutes**, until set. Let them cool on the sheet for a
      minute, then move them to a rack.

notes:
  - Dip the glass in sugar between cookies so it doesn't stick.
  - Bake time covers about two batches. Total time matches the source's 2 hr 5 min.
---
