---
title: White Chicken Chili
summary: >-
  A creamy, warming chili with rotisserie chicken, cannellini beans, green
  chilies, and corn. A lighter change from beef chili.
category: Soup
# image: /assets/images/recipes/white-chicken-chili.jpg
# image_alt: Bowl of white chicken chili topped with avocado and cilantro
time:
  prep: 15
  cook: 35
servings: 6
yield: About 8 cups
source:
  name: Cooking Classy
  author: Jaclyn
  url: https://www.cookingclassy.com/white-chicken-chili/

ingredients:
  - group: Base
    items:
      - qty: "1 tablespoon"
        item: olive oil
      - qty: "1 small"
        item: yellow onion, diced
      - qty: "2 cloves"
        item: garlic, finely minced
      - qty: "2 cans (14.5 ounces each)"
        item: low-sodium chicken broth
      - qty: "1 can (7 ounces)"
        item: diced green chilies
  - group: Spices
    items:
      - qty: "1½ teaspoons"
        item: cumin
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: paprika
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: dried oregano
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: ground coriander
      - qty: "¼ teaspoon"
        item: cayenne pepper
      - qty: ""
        item: salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  - group: Chili
    items:
      - qty: "1 package (8 ounces)"
        item: Neufchâtel (light cream cheese), cut into small cubes and softened
      - qty: "1¼ cups"
        item: frozen or fresh corn
      - qty: "2 cans (15 ounces each)"
        item: cannellini beans
      - qty: "2½ cups"
        item: shredded cooked chicken (rotisserie or leftover)
      - qty: "1 tablespoon"
        item: fresh lime juice
      - qty: "2 tablespoons"
        item: chopped fresh cilantro, plus more for serving
  - group: For serving (optional)
    items:
      - qty: ""
        item: tortilla chips or strips, Monterey Jack, sliced avocado

steps:
  - title: Sauté the aromatics
    text: >-
      Heat the oil in a large pot over **medium-high heat**. Sauté the onion for
      **4 minutes**, then add the garlic and cook **30 seconds** more.
  - title: Simmer the broth
    text: >-
      Add the broth, green chilies, cumin, paprika, oregano, coriander, and
      cayenne, and season with salt and pepper. Bring just to a boil, then turn
      to **medium-low** and simmer **15 minutes**.
  - title: Purée some beans
    text: >-
      Drain and rinse the beans. Put **1 cup** of them in a food processor with
      **¼ cup** of broth from the pot and purée until nearly smooth. Keep the rest
      whole.
  - title: Add the cheese, corn, and beans
    text: >-
      Stir the Neufchâtel, corn, whole beans, and puréed beans into the pot.
      Simmer **5–10 minutes** more.
  - title: Finish
    text: >-
      Stir in the chicken, lime juice, and cilantro. Serve with Monterey Jack,
      extra cilantro, avocado, and tortilla chips if you like.

notes:
  - Soften the Neufchâtel first so it doesn't curdle. Regular cream cheese works too; use 7 ounces and pulse it with the beans in the food processor.
  - No food processor? Mash the beans with a potato masher, or skip that step. The chili will just be less creamy.
  - Freezes in an airtight container for up to 3 months.
