---
title: Cannoli Dip
summary: >-
  All the filling of a cannoli, none of the work: sweet whipped ricotta and
  cream cheese with mini chocolate chips, served with broken waffle cones.
category: Dessert
# image: /assets/images/recipes/cannoli-dip.jpg
# image_alt: Bowl of cannoli dip with chocolate chips and waffle cone pieces
time:
  prep: 10
  cook: 0
servings: 8
calories: 286
source:
  name: Homemade Hooplah
  author: Chrisy Toombs
  url: https://homemadehooplah.com/cannoli-dip/

ingredients:
  - group: Dip
    items:
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: ricotta
      - qty: "8 ounces"
        item: cream cheese, softened
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: powdered sugar
      - qty: "1 teaspoon"
        item: vanilla extract
      - qty: "⅔ cup"
        item: mini chocolate chips
  - group: For dipping
    items:
      - qty: ""
        item: waffle cones, broken into dippable pieces

steps:
  - title: Whip the base
    text: >-
      Beat the ricotta, cream cheese, and powdered sugar with a stand or hand mixer
      on **medium-high** until smooth, **3–5 minutes**.
  - title: Add the vanilla
    text: Add the vanilla and whip **30 seconds** more.
  - title: Taste
    text: >-
      For a sweeter dip, beat in more powdered sugar **2 tablespoons** at a time,
      up to **½ cup** more.
  - title: Fold in the chips
    text: >-
      Fold in the chocolate chips, saving a few to sprinkle on top. Serve with
      broken waffle cones.

notes:
  - No mixer? Whisk it by hand. It just takes more elbow grease.
---
