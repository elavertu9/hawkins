ingredients:

    items:
      - qty: "3–4 pounds"
        item: boneless chuck roast
      - qty: ""
        item: kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

      - qty: "¼–½ cup"
        item: all-purpose flour, as needed (optional)
      - qty: "3 tablespoons"
        item: olive oil
  - group: Slow cooker
    items:

        item: beef broth
      - qty: "1 packet"

        item: unsalted butter

  - group: For serving
    items:
      - qty: ""
        item: bread, cooked egg noodles, or potatoes

steps:
  - title: Season the roast
    text: >-
      Season the roast generously with salt and pepper, then rub on just enough
      flour to coat the outside evenly.
  - title: Sear
    text: >-
      Heat the oil in a large skillet over **high heat** until very hot but not
      smoking. Sear the roast on all sides until browned and crusty, about
      **10 minutes** total.
  - title: Load the slow cooker
    text: >-
      Put the roast in the slow cooker and pour the broth around it. Sprinkle the
      ranch seasoning over the top, set the butter on it, and scatter the
      pepperoncini over and around.
  - title: Slow cook
    text: >-
      Cover and cook on **LOW** for **7–8 hours**, until the beef is fork-tender.
  - title: Shred and serve
    text: >-
      Shred the meat right in the slow cooker and stir it into the sauce. Serve
      over bread, egg noodles, or potatoes.

notes:
  - "Short on time: skip the flour and searing, and add a 1-ounce packet of dry au jus gravy mix with the ranch instead."
  - Calorie estimate assumes a 3½-pound roast and doesn't include the bread, noodles, or potatoes.
  - Leftovers make great sandwiches, tacos, or sliders.
