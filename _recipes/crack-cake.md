---
title: Crack Cake
summary: >-
  A cinnamon-spiced bundt cake from a boxed mix and vanilla pudding, soaked
  while hot with a buttery white-wine sugar glaze. Dangerously addictive.
category: Dessert
# image: /assets/images/recipes/crack-cake.jpg
# image_alt: Glazed golden bundt cake on a plate
time:
  prep: 5
  cook: 60
servings: 16
calories: 365
calories_estimated: true
yield: 1 bundt cake
source:
  name: The Sarcastic Blonde
  author: Taylor
  url: https://www.thesarcasticblonde.com/2012/05/monogram-monday-crack-cake.html

ingredients:
  - group: Cake
    items:
      - qty: "1 box"
        item: yellow cake mix (such as Duncan Hines)
      - qty: "1 box"
        item: instant vanilla pudding mix
      - qty: "¼ cup"
        item: brown sugar
      - qty: "¼ cup"
        item: white sugar
      - qty: "2 teaspoons"
        item: cinnamon
      - qty: "4"
        item: eggs
      - qty: "¾ cup"
        item: water
      - qty: "¾ cup"
        item: oil
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: white wine (any kind; pinot grigio works well)
  - group: Glaze
    items:
      - qty: "1 stick"
        item: butter
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: sugar
      - qty: "¼ cup"
        item: white wine

steps:
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: >-
      Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Grease a bundt pan very well with butter or
      baking spray.
  - title: Mix the batter
    text: >-
      Mix all the cake ingredients by hand or with a hand mixer until smooth.
  - title: Bake
    text: >-
      Pour the batter into the pan and bake for about **1 hour**, until a knife
      comes out clean.
  - title: Make the glaze
    text: >-
      While the cake bakes, melt the butter with the sugar and wine in a small
      saucepan, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
  - title: Glaze the hot cake
    text: >-
      As soon as the cake comes out of the oven, poke holes in it and pour the
      glaze over so it soaks in.
  - title: Cool and unmold
    text: >-
      Let it cool in the pan. Loosen the edges with a flexible spatula, then flip
      it out onto a plate, giving the pan a few firm shakes so it releases in
      one piece.

notes:
  - The source says it serves "1 cake". Serves 16 assumes 16 slices.
  - Contains wine. The glaze isn't cooked long, so some alcohol remains.
  - Red velvet, chocolate, or Funfetti cake mix all work in place of yellow.
