---
title: Egg Roll in a Bowl
summary: >-
  All the flavor of an egg roll without the wrapper: ground pork, cabbage, and
  carrots in a sesame-hoisin sauce, in one skillet and 20 minutes.
category: Dinner
# image: /assets/images/recipes/egg-roll-in-a-bowl.jpg
# image_alt: Skillet of ground pork and cabbage stir-fry
time:
  prep: 5
  cook: 15
servings: 4
calories: 234
source:
  name: The Seasoned Mom
  author: Blair Lonergan
  url: https://www.theseasonedmom.com/egg-roll-in-a-bowl/

ingredients:
  - group: Skillet
    items:
      - qty: "1 pound"
        item: ground pork (or ground turkey, chicken, or beef)
      - qty: "1 small"
        item: sweet onion, diced
      - qty: "1 tablespoon"
        item: toasted sesame oil
      - qty: "1 tablespoon"
        item: rice vinegar
      - qty: "¼ cup"
        item: less-sodium soy sauce
      - qty: "2 tablespoons"
        item: hoisin sauce (add about 1 tablespoon more for a sweeter, richer sauce)
      - qty: "2 teaspoons"
        item: minced fresh garlic
      - qty: "1 teaspoon"
        item: ground ginger (or 1½ tablespoons grated fresh ginger)
      - qty: "1 bag (16 ounces)"
        item: coleslaw mix
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: grated or matchstick carrots
      - qty: "2"
        item: green onions, thinly sliced
      - qty: ""
        item: kosher salt and ground black pepper, to taste
  - group: Optional garnish
    items:
      - qty: ""
        item: >-
          sliced green onions, chives, cilantro, toasted sesame seeds, chopped
          peanuts, crunchy chow mein noodles, or a squeeze of lime

steps:
  - title: Brown the meat
    text: >-
      Heat a large skillet over **medium-high heat**. Add the ground meat and
      cook, stirring, until no longer pink, **5–7 minutes**. Drain off the grease
      and return the meat to the pan.
  - title: Soften the onion
    text: >-
      Add the onion, sesame oil, and rice vinegar. Cook, stirring, until the onion
      is tender, **4–5 minutes**.
  - title: Add the sauce and vegetables
    text: >-
      Add the soy sauce, hoisin, garlic, ginger, coleslaw mix, and carrots. Cook,
      stirring, until the cabbage wilts, **5–7 minutes**.
  - title: Finish
    text: >-
      Take the skillet off the heat. Stir in the green onions and season with salt
      and pepper. Serve with any garnishes you like.

notes:
  - Don't skip the toasted sesame oil. It's what makes it taste like an egg roll.
  - Good add-ins are diced celery, bell peppers, snap peas, snow peas, or baby corn.
  - Reheat single servings in the microwave for about 1 minute.
