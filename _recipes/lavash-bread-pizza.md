---
title: Lavash Bread Pizza
summary: >-
  A thin, crispy pizza on lavash flatbread with crushed tomatoes, mozzarella,
  and turkey pepperoni. About 350 calories for the whole thing.
category: Dinner
image: /assets/images/recipes/lavash-bread-pizza.jpg
image_alt: Rectangular lavash pizza with pepperoni and melted mozzarella on parchment
tags: [Low Calorie, Quick]
added: 2026-11-03
time:
  prep: 5
  cook: 15
servings: 1
calories: 346
yield: 1 pizza

ingredients:
  - items:
      - qty: "1"
        item: lavash flatbread
      - qty: "⅓ cup"
        item: crushed tomatoes
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: shredded mozzarella
      - qty: "12 slices"
        item: turkey pepperoni
      - qty: ""
        item: Italian seasoning, for sprinkling

steps:
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: >-
      Preheat the oven to **350°F** and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  - title: Build the pizza
    text: >-
      Lay the lavash on the baking sheet. Spread the crushed tomatoes over it,
      then top with the mozzarella and the pepperoni, and sprinkle with Italian
      seasoning.
  - title: Bake
    text: >-
      Bake **10 to 15 minutes**, until the cheese is melted and the edges are
      crisp.

notes:
  - "Calories by ingredient: lavash 100, crushed tomatoes 30, mozzarella 160, and 12 slices of turkey pepperoni 56."
