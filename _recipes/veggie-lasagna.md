---
title: Veggie Lasagna
summary: >-
  A meatless 9x13 lasagna with lightly sautéed zucchini, summer squash, and
  mushrooms layered between seasoned tomato sauce and a creamy ricotta filling.
category: Dinner
# image: /assets/images/recipes/veggie-lasagna.jpg
# image_alt: Slice of vegetable lasagna with zucchini and squash
time:
  prep: 35
  cook: 55
  additional: 10
servings: 12
calories: 430
calories_estimated: true
yield: 1 (9x13-inch) pan

ingredients:
  - group: Sauce
    items:
      - qty: "1–1½ jars"
        item: tomato basil pasta sauce (such as Classico)
      - qty: "1 can (about 28 ounces)"
        item: crushed tomatoes
      - qty: ""
        item: salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and dried basil, to taste
  - group: Vegetables
    items:
      - qty: "2"
        item: zucchini, cut into ½-inch slices
      - qty: "2"
        item: summer squash, cut into ½-inch slices
      - qty: "1 package"
        item: sliced mushrooms
      - qty: ""
        item: olive oil, plus salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and dried basil
  - group: Ricotta filling
    items:
      - qty: "1 container (30 ounces)"
        item: whole-milk ricotta (such as Sargento or Frigo)
      - qty: "2"
        item: eggs
      - qty: "2 cups"
        item: shredded 5-cheese Italian blend
      - qty: "2 cups"
        item: chopped fresh spinach (optional)
  - group: Layers and topping
    items:
      - qty: "1 package"
        item: no-boil lasagna noodles (such as Barilla)
      - qty: ""
        item: shredded mozzarella or 5-cheese Italian blend, for the top

steps:
  - title: Simmer the sauce
    text: >-
      Combine the jarred sauce and crushed tomatoes in a saucepan and season with
      salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and basil. Simmer while you
      prepare the vegetables.
  - title: Sauté the vegetables
    text: >-
      Sauté the zucchini, squash, and mushrooms in a little olive oil with the
      seasonings, just briefly. They should stay firm, since they'll keep cooking
      in the oven.
  - title: Make the ricotta filling
    text: >-
      Mix the ricotta, eggs, shredded cheese, and spinach if using. Keep it in the
      fridge until you're ready to layer.
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: Preheat the oven to **375°F**.
  - title: Start the layers
    text: >-
      Spread a thin layer of sauce over the bottom of a **9x13-inch** pan. Cover it
      with a layer of no-boil noodles.
  - title: Layer
    text: >-
      Spread on **⅓** of the ricotta filling, add a layer of vegetables, then
      **⅓** of the sauce, then another layer of noodles. Repeat until everything is
      used, about **3 layers**.
  - title: Top and cover
    text: >-
      Spread a thin layer of sauce over the top noodles, then cover the pan with
      foil.
  - title: Bake covered
    text: Bake **45 minutes**.
  - title: Add the cheese and brown
    text: >-
      Remove the foil, cover the top with mozzarella or 5-cheese blend, and bake
      **10 minutes** more, until the cheese is lightly browned.
  - title: Rest
    text: Let it cool **10 minutes** before cutting.

notes:
  - Family recipe. The original doesn't list times or servings. Those are estimates.
  - For extra flavor, add sliced hard deli cheese blends with the shredded cheese, and sprinkle salt, pepper, garlic, onion, and basil over the layers as you build.
  - Calorie estimate assumes 1¼ jars of sauce and about 2 cups of cheese on top, without the optional spinach.
  - Want meat? See [Meat Lasagna](/recipes/meat-lasagna/).
