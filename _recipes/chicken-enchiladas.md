  One big chicken enchilada for one: sautéed chicken, onion, and grape tomatoes

  About 520 calories.

  prep: 10
  cook: 30

  name: College Nutritionist

ingredients:
  - items:
      - qty: "6 ounces"
        item: boneless, skinless chicken breast
        shop: boneless skinless chicken breasts
      - qty: "½"
        item: yellow onion, diced
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: grape tomatoes, diced
      - qty: "1 large"
        item: soft flour tortilla
      - qty: "¼ cup"
        item: enchilada sauce, divided
      - qty: "⅓ cup"
        item: shredded cheddar cheese
      - qty: ""
        item: a dash each of salt and pepper
        shop: [salt, black pepper]
      - qty: ""
        item: cooking spray

steps:
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: >-
      Preheat the oven to **350°F**.
  - title: Prep the filling
    text: >-
      Dice the onion and tomatoes, and cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces.
  - title: Cook the filling
    text: >-
      Coat a skillet with cooking spray. Cook the chicken, onion, and tomatoes
      over **medium heat** until the chicken is cooked through, about **7
      minutes**.
  - title: Fill and roll
    text: |-
      - Spoon the filling down the middle of the tortilla.
      - Season with the salt and pepper and add 2 tablespoons of the enchilada
        sauce.
      - Roll it up tightly.
  - title: Top and bake
    text: |-
      - Coat a small baking dish with cooking spray and set the enchilada in it,
        seam side down.
      - Spoon the rest of the enchilada sauce over it and sprinkle with the cheddar.
      - Bake **20 minutes**, until the cheese is melted.

notes:
  - It's easiest to make 2 or 3 at once. Multiply everything and bake them side by side in one dish.
  - No chicken? Use tofu, fish, or beans. For dairy-free, use a dairy-free cheese.
  - "Calories by ingredient: tortilla 110, enchilada sauce 24, chicken 190, onion 28, grape tomatoes 16, and cheddar 152."
