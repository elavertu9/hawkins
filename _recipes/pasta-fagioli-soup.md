steps:
  - title: Brown the beef
    text: >-
      Heat **1 tablespoon** of the olive oil in a large pot over
      **medium-high heat**. Crumble in the beef and cook, stirring now and then,
      until cooked through.
  - title: Drain and set aside
    text: >-
      Drain off the fat and move the beef to a plate. Add the remaining
      **1 tablespoon** of oil to the same pot.
  - title: Cook the vegetables
    text: >-
      Add the onion, carrots, and celery and sauté over **medium-high heat**
      until tender, about **6 minutes**. Add the garlic and cook **1 minute**
      more.
  - title: Build the soup
    text: >-
      Pour in the broth, tomato sauce, water, and diced tomatoes. Add the sugar,
      basil, oregano, thyme, marjoram, and the cooked beef, then season with salt
      and pepper.
  - title: Simmer
    text: >-
      Bring to a boil, then turn the heat to **medium-low**, cover, and simmer,
      stirring occasionally, until the vegetables are soft, **15–20 minutes**.
  - title: Cook the pasta
    text: >-
      While the soup simmers, cook the ditalini in a separate pot until al dente,
      following the package directions. Drain.
  - title: Add the pasta and beans
    text: >-
      Stir the pasta, kidney beans, and great northern beans into the soup. Thin
      with a little more broth or water if you like.
  - title: Finish and serve
    text: >-
      Cook **1 minute** longer, then stir in the parsley. Serve warm with Romano
      or Parmesan.

notes:
  - If you won't eat it all right away, keep the pasta separate and add it to each bowl. Otherwise it soaks up the broth and goes soggy.
