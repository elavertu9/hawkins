---
title: Banana Bread
summary: >-
  A moist, brown sugar banana bread made with overripe bananas, with optional
  mini chocolate chips.
category: Breakfast
tags: [Vegetarian]
added: 2026-10-24
# image: /assets/images/recipes/banana-bread.jpg
# image_alt: Sliced loaf of banana bread
time:
  prep: 15
  cook: 55
  additional: 10
servings: 12
calories: 240
calories_estimated: true
yield: 1 (9x5-inch) loaf

ingredients:
  - group: Dry
    items:
      - qty: "2 cups"
        item: all-purpose flour
      - qty: "1 teaspoon"
        item: baking soda
      - qty: "¼ teaspoon"
        item: salt
  - group: Wet
    items:
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: butter, room temperature
      - qty: "¾ cup"
        item: brown sugar
      - qty: "2"
        item: eggs, beaten
      - qty: "3–4"
        item: overripe bananas, mashed
  - group: Optional
    items:
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: mini semi-sweet chocolate chips

steps:
  - title: Heat the oven and prep the pan
    text: >-
      Preheat the oven to **375°F**. Line the bottom of a **9x5-inch** loaf pan
      with a strip of parchment, grease it with shortening, and dust with cocoa
      powder or flour.
  - title: Mix the dry ingredients
    text: In a large bowl, combine the flour, baking soda, and salt.
  - title: Mix the wet ingredients
    text: >-
      In another bowl, cream the butter and brown sugar. Stir in the eggs and
      bananas until well blended.
  - title: Combine
    text: >-
      Stir the banana mixture into the flour mixture just until combined. Pour into
      the pan and stir in the chocolate chips, if using.
  - title: Bake
    text: >-
      Bake **50–60 minutes**, until a toothpick in the center comes out clean.
      Baking times vary with elevation.
  - title: Cool
    text: >-
      Cool in the pan for **10 minutes**, then turn it out onto a wire rack.

notes:
  - Family recipe. The original doesn't list servings or prep time. Those are estimates.
  - Calorie estimate is for 12 slices without chocolate chips. Chips add about 35 per slice.
  - The darker and spottier the bananas, the sweeter the bread.
