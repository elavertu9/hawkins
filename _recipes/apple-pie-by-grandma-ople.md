---
title: Apple Pie by Grandma Ople
summary: >-
  Tart Granny Smith apples under a lattice crust, with a buttery brown sugar
  sauce poured over the top that bakes into a glossy, caramelized finish.
category: Dessert
# image: /assets/images/recipes/apple-pie-by-grandma-ople.jpg
# image_alt: Lattice-topped apple pie
time:
  prep: 30
  cook: 60
servings: 8
calories: 373
yield: 1 (9-inch) pie
source:
  name: Allrecipes
  author: MOSHASMAMA
  url: https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/12682/apple-pie-by-grandma-ople/

ingredients:
  - group: Filling
    items:
      - qty: "8 small"
        item: Granny Smith apples, or as needed (about 6 cups sliced)
  - group: Sauce
    items:
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: unsalted butter
      - qty: "3 tablespoons"
        item: all-purpose flour
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: white sugar
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: packed brown sugar
      - qty: "¼ cup"
        item: water
  - group: Crust
    items:
      - qty: "1"
        item: (9-inch) double-crust pie pastry, thawed

steps:
  - title: Heat the oven and slice the apples
    text: >-
      Preheat the oven to **425°F (220°C)**. Peel and core the apples, slice them
      thinly and evenly, and set them aside.
  - title: Start the sauce
    text: >-
      Melt the butter in a saucepan over **medium heat**. Stir in the flour to
      make a paste and cook until it smells toasty, **1 to 2 minutes**.
  - title: Finish the sauce
    text: >-
      Add both sugars and the water and bring to a boil. Turn the heat to low,
      simmer for **3 to 5 minutes**, then take it off the heat.
  - title: Line the pan
    text: >-
      Press one crust into the bottom and up the sides of a 9-inch pie pan.
  - title: Cut the lattice strips
    text: >-
      Roll out the second crust so it's big enough to hang about ½ inch past the
      edge of the pie. Cut it into **eight 1-inch strips**.
  - title: Add the apples
    text: >-
      Pile the apple slices into the crust, mounding them slightly in the middle.
  - title: Weave the lattice
    text: >-
      Lay four strips across the pie in one direction, evenly spaced, with the
      longest strips in the middle. Fold back the 1st and 3rd strips, lay a strip
      across the other way, and fold them back down. Then fold back the 2nd and
      4th strips, lay the next crosswise strip, and fold them down. Repeat until
      all eight strips are woven in.
  - title: Seal the edges
    text: >-
      Trim any extra pastry and pinch the edges together to seal.
  - title: Pour on the sauce
    text: >-
      Slowly pour the sauce over the lattice so it soaks down through the gaps
      into the apples. Brush a little over the pastry, keeping it from running
      over the sides.
  - title: Bake hot, then lower the heat
    text: >-
      Bake for **15 minutes**. Lower the oven to **350°F (175°C)** and bake another
      **35 to 45 minutes**, until the crust is golden, the filling is bubbling
      through the lattice, and the apples are tender. If the edges brown too fast,
      cover them loosely with foil.
  - title: Let it set
    text: >-
      Cool the pie on a wire rack for about **2 hours** before slicing so the
      filling can set. Reheat slices if you like it warm.

notes:
  - Set a baking sheet on the rack below the pie to catch any sauce that bubbles over.
  - Granny Smiths hold their shape and balance the sweet sauce. Mixing in a firm, sweeter apple like Honeycrisp works too.
  - Serve warm with ice cream or whipped cream, or the old-school way, with a slice of sharp Cheddar.
  - Keeps covered at room temperature for 2 days, in the fridge for 4 days, or frozen for up to 3 months. Reheat in a 350°F oven for 15 to 20 minutes.
