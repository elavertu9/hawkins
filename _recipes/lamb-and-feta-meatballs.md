  Greek-style beef and lamb meatballs with feta, lemon zest, oregano, cumin, and
  coriander, baked and served with tzatziki.

  prep: 15

ingredients:
  - group: Meat
    items:
      - qty: "½ pound"
        item: ground beef
      - qty: "½ pound"
        item: ground lamb
      - qty: "1"
        item: egg, beaten
      - qty: "3 ounces"
        item: crumbled feta cheese
  - group: Flavorings
    items:
      - qty: "¼ cup"

      - qty: "2 tablespoons"

      - qty: "3 tablespoons"
        item: minced onion (or 1½ teaspoons onion powder)
      - qty: "2 cloves"
        item: garlic, minced
      - qty: "1"

      - qty: "1 teaspoon"

      - qty: "½ teaspoon"

      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: cumin
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: coarse salt
      - qty: "¼ teaspoon"
        item: pepper

steps:
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: >-
      Preheat the oven to **425°F** and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  - title: Mix
    text: >-
      Put everything in a large bowl and mix gently with your hands until evenly
      combined.
  - title: Shape
    text: >-
      Divide the mixture into **4** equal parts, then each part into **5**
      meatballs, for about **20**. Roll them with wet hands and set them on the
      baking sheet.
  - title: Bake
    text: >-
      Bake **12–15 minutes**, rotating the pan once, until cooked through.

notes:

  - "For a party platter: meatballs with toothpicks, a bowl of tzatziki, warm pitas, tomatoes, onions, olives, cucumber slices, and a brick of feta drizzled with olive oil."
