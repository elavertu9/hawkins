---
title: Fruit Salad
summary: >-
  A big summer bowl of pineapple, watermelon, berries, grapes, and mandarin
  oranges, cut bite-size and tossed in a splash of mandarin juice.
category: Side
tags: [Quick, Crowd, Vegetarian]
added: 2026-10-24
# image: /assets/images/recipes/fruit-salad.jpg
# image_alt: Large bowl of mixed fresh fruit
time:
  prep: 30
  cook: 0
servings: 20
calories: 125
calories_estimated: true
yield: About 25 cups

ingredients:
  - items:
      - qty: "1"
        item: pineapple
      - qty: "1 small (or ½ regular)"
        item: watermelon
      - qty: "1 pint"
        item: strawberries
      - qty: "1 pint"
        item: blueberries
      - qty: "1 pound"
        item: grapes, halved
      - qty: "1½ cans"
        item: mandarin oranges, juice drained into a separate bowl and saved

steps:
  - title: Cut the fruit
    text: >-
      Cut the pineapple, watermelon, strawberries, and grapes into small bite-size
      pieces, about **¼ inch**. Leave the blueberries and mandarin oranges whole.
  - title: Mix
    text: Toss all the fruit together in a large bowl.
  - title: Add the juice
    text: >-
      Pour in **¼–½ cup** of the saved mandarin juice and toss again.
  - title: Chill
    text: Cover and refrigerate until serving.

notes:
  - Family recipe. The original doesn't list times or servings. Those are estimates, with a serving of about 1¼ cups.
