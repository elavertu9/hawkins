  A hearty soup of sweet Italian sausage, tomatoes, red wine, and vegetables,
  with tender cheese tortellini added at the end.

ingredients:
  - group: Soup
    items:
      - qty: "1 pound"
        item: sweet Italian sausage, casings removed
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: chopped onion
      - qty: "2 cloves"
        item: garlic, minced
      - qty: "5 cups"
        item: beef broth
      - qty: "4 large"
        item: tomatoes, peeled, seeded, and chopped
      - qty: "1 can (8 ounces)"
        item: tomato sauce
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: thinly sliced carrots
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: water
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: red wine
      - qty: "½ tablespoon"
        item: packed fresh basil leaves
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: dried oregano
  - group: To finish
    items:
      - qty: "1½ cups"
        item: sliced zucchini
      - qty: "3 tablespoons"
        item: chopped fresh parsley
      - qty: "8 ounces"
        item: fresh tortellini

steps:
  - title: Brown the sausage
    text: >-
      Cook the sausage in a 5-quart Dutch oven over **medium-high heat** until
      browned and crumbly, **8–10 minutes**. Lift it out with a slotted spoon onto
      a paper towel–lined plate. Pour off all but **1 tablespoon** of the fat.
  - title: Soften the onion and garlic
    text: >-
      Sauté the onion and garlic in the drippings over **medium heat** until the
      onion is translucent, **3–4 minutes**.
  - title: Simmer the soup
    text: >-
      Stir in the broth, tomatoes, tomato sauce, carrots, water, wine, basil,
      oregano, and the sausage. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer
      uncovered for **30 minutes**.
  - title: Add the zucchini
    text: >-
      Skim the fat off the top. Stir in the zucchini and parsley, cover, and
      simmer **30 minutes** more.
  - title: Add the tortellini
    text: >-
      Add the tortellini for the last **10 minutes** of simmering. Serve hot.

notes:
  - Good with grated Parmesan on top and crusty bread on the side.
