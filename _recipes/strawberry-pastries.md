  Crisp puff pastry squares with an almond-sugar top, split and filled with
  fresh strawberries and whipped topping.

  name: Pampered Chef

ingredients:
  - group: Pastry
    items:
      - qty: "½ package (17.3 oz/450 g)"
        item: frozen puff pastry sheets (1 sheet), thawed
      - qty: "¼ cup (50 mL)"
        item: sliced almonds, finely chopped
      - qty: "1 tablespoon (15 mL)"
        item: sugar
  - group: Filling
    items:
      - qty: "1½ cups (375 mL)"
        item: frozen whipped topping, thawed
        shop: frozen whipped topping
      - qty: "12 large"
        item: strawberries, sliced

steps:
  - title: Heat the oven and top the pastry
    text: >-
      Preheat the oven to **400°F (200°C)**. Unfold the pastry sheet onto a baking
      stone or baking sheet. Mix the almonds and sugar, sprinkle them over the
      pastry, and press them in lightly.
  - title: Cut into squares
    text: >-
      With a pizza cutter or sharp knife, cut the pastry lengthwise into 3 strips,
      then cut each strip crosswise into 4 squares, for **12** in all. Spread the
      squares out so they have room to puff.
  - title: Bake and cool
    text: >-
      Bake for **16–18 minutes**, until golden brown. Let them cool completely,
      or the filling will melt.
  - title: Fill
    text: >-
      Split each square in half horizontally. Lay half the strawberries on the
      bottoms, add the whipped topping and the rest of the strawberries, then set
      the tops back on.

notes:
  - The source doesn't list prep or cool times. These are estimates.
  - Fill them just before serving so the pastry stays crisp.
