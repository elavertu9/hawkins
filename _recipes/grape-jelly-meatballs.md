---
title: Grape Jelly Meatballs
summary: >-
  The classic three-ingredient party meatballs: frozen meatballs slow-cooked in
  a sweet and tangy grape jelly and chili sauce glaze.
category: Appetizer
# image: /assets/images/recipes/grape-jelly-meatballs.jpg
# image_alt: Glazed meatballs in a slow cooker
time:
  prep: 5
  cook: 240
servings: 15
calories: 290
source:
  name: The Magical Slow Cooker
  author: Sarah Olson
  url: https://www.themagicalslowcooker.com/slow-cooker-grape-jelly-meatballs/

ingredients:
  - items:
      - qty: "1 bag (32 ounces)"
        item: frozen meatballs (homestyle works well)
      - qty: "20 ounces"
        item: grape jelly (such as Welch's)
      - qty: "12 ounces"
        item: Heinz chili sauce

steps:
  - title: Combine
    text: >-
      Put the frozen meatballs in a **5-quart or larger** slow cooker. Add the
      grape jelly and chili sauce and stir until the meatballs are coated.
  - title: Slow cook
    text: >-
      Cover and cook on **LOW** for **4–5 hours** or **HIGH** for
      **2½–3 hours**, stirring now and then so the edges don't burn.
  - title: Keep warm and serve
    text: >-
      Switch the slow cooker to **Warm** for serving. Set out toothpicks.

notes:
  - "Stovetop: heat everything in a large pot over medium-low for about 15 minutes, until the meatballs are hot and the sauce starts to thicken."
  - They'll hold on Warm for 3+ hours at a party. Stir often, and add a splash of water if the sauce gets too thick.
