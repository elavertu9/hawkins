  Thin, tender crêpes from ingredients you already have, ready in minutes. Fill
  them sweet or savory.

  prep: 10

yield: 8 crêpes

  name: Allrecipes

ingredients:
  - items:
      - qty: "2 large"
        item: eggs
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: milk, or more as needed
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: water
      - qty: "¼ teaspoon"
        item: salt, or to taste
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: all-purpose flour
      - qty: "2 tablespoons"
        item: butter, melted

steps:
  - title: Make the batter
    text: >-
      Whisk the eggs, milk, water, and salt in a large bowl. Add the flour and
      melted butter and whisk hard until the batter is smooth and pourable.
  - title: Heat the pan
    text: >-
      Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over **medium-high heat**.
  - title: Pour and swirl
    text: >-
      Pour in about **¼ cup** of batter and tilt the pan in a circle so it coats
      the surface in a thin, even layer.
  - title: Cook the first side
    text: >-
      Cook until the top is no longer wet and the bottom is light brown,
      **1–2 minutes**.
  - title: Flip and finish
    text: >-
      Loosen the edge with a spatula, flip, and cook until the other side is light
      brown, about **1 minute** more. Serve hot.

notes:
  - If the batter is too thick to spread, whisk in milk 1 tablespoon at a time.
  - "Sweet crêpes: whisk 2 tablespoons sugar and 1 teaspoon vanilla into the batter."
  - You can also blend everything in a blender with a few short pulses.
