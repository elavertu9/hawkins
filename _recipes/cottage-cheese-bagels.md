---
title: Cottage Cheese Bagels
summary: >-
  Four-ingredient, high-protein bagels made from blended cottage cheese and
  flour, brushed with egg and topped with everything bagel seasoning.
category: Breakfast
tags: [Low Calorie, Vegetarian]
added: 2026-10-26
image: /assets/images/recipes/cottage-cheese-bagels.jpg
image_alt: Golden cottage cheese bagels topped with everything seasoning
time:
  prep: 15
  cook: 20
servings: 4
calories: 186
yield: 4 bagels

ingredients:
  - group: Dough
    items:
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: cottage cheese, blended smooth
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: all-purpose flour, plus more for the counter
      - qty: "1 teaspoon"
        item: baking powder
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: salt
  - group: Topping
    items:
      - qty: "1"
        item: egg, whisked, for the egg wash
      - qty: ""
        item: everything bagel seasoning

steps:
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: >-
      Preheat the oven to **375°F** and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  - title: Make the dough
    text: >-
      Stir the blended cottage cheese, flour, baking powder, and salt together in
      a bowl until a thick dough forms.
  - title: Divide
    text: >-
      Turn the dough out onto a floured counter and cut it into **4** equal
      pieces.
  - title: Shape
    text: >-
      Roll each piece into a ball, flatten it into a thick disk, and poke a hole
      through the middle with your finger. Set the bagels on the baking sheet.
  - title: Top
    text: >-
      Brush the bagels with the egg wash and sprinkle with everything bagel
      seasoning.
  - title: Bake
    text: Bake **20 minutes**, until golden brown.

notes:
  - Calories are per bagel, from the original recipe, and don't include toppings. Two tablespoons of cream cheese adds about 100 (about 60 for light).
  - Blend the cottage cheese in a blender or food processor first so the dough comes out smooth.
  - The original doesn't list a prep time. That's an estimate.
