---
title: Jalapeño Cheese Dip
summary: >-
  A creamy, cheesy jalapeño and green chili dip baked in a sourdough bread bowl.
  Always the first thing gone at a cookout.
category: Appetizer
# image: /assets/images/recipes/jalapeno-cheese-dip.jpg
# image_alt: Cheese dip baked in a sourdough bread bowl
time:
  prep: 10
  cook: 30
servings: 8
calories: 438
yield: 2 cups
source:
  name: Allrecipes
  author: happywife05
  url: https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/203218/insanely-amazing-jalapeno-cheese-dip/

ingredients:
  - items:
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: mayonnaise
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: shredded Parmesan cheese
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: shredded cheddar cheese
      - qty: "1 can (4 ounces)"
        item: diced jalapeño peppers
      - qty: "1 can (4 ounces)"
        item: chopped green chilies
      - qty: "1 round loaf"
        item: sourdough bread

steps:
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: Preheat the oven to **350°F (175°C)**.
  - title: Mix the dip
    text: >-
      In a bowl, stir together the mayonnaise, Parmesan, cheddar, jalapeños, and
      green chilies.
  - title: Make the bread bowl
    text: >-
      Slice the top off the sourdough round and hollow out the center, leaving
      sturdy walls. Spoon the dip inside.
  - title: Bake
    text: >-
      Bake until hot all the way through and golden on top, about
      **30 minutes**.

notes:
  - Cube the bread you scooped out and toast it alongside for dipping. Crackers and chips work too.
