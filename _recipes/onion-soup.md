  Buttery, slow-simmered onion soup made easy with canned consommé, ladled over
  croutons and broiled under a blanket of melted Parmesan.
category: Soup

# image_alt: Bowl of onion soup with broiled Parmesan on top
time:
  prep: 10

ingredients:
  - group: Soup
    items:
      - qty: "1 stick"
        item: butter
      - qty: "2–3"
        item: onions, sliced thin (a mix of kinds gives better flavor)
      - qty: "3–4 cans"
        item: consommé
      - qty: "½ can per can of consommé"
        item: water
  - group: To serve
    items:
      - qty: ""
        item: croutons, dried bread, or toast
        shop: croutons
      - qty: ""
        item: shredded Parmesan cheese

steps:
  - title: Cook the onions
    text: >-
      Melt the butter in a large pot. Add the onions and cook, stirring
      occasionally, until they turn clear.
  - title: Simmer
    text: >-
      Add the consommé and water and simmer for at least **1 hour**.
  - title: Fill the bowls
    text: >-
      Turn on the broiler. Ladle a small amount of soup into each oven-safe bowl
      and add croutons, dried bread, or toast.
  - title: Broil
    text: >-
      Top with shredded Parmesan and set the bowls under the broiler until the
      cheese is melted and browned.

notes:
  - Family recipe, from a handwritten card. It doesn't list times or servings. Those are estimates.
  - Calorie estimate is per bowl, with croutons and about ¼ cup Parmesan, assuming 3½ cans of consommé.
  - Use oven-safe bowls for the broiler, and set them on a baking sheet so they're easy to move.
