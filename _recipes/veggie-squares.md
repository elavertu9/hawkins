ingredients:
  - group: Crust
    items:
      - qty: "2 cans"
        item: crescent rolls
  - group: Spread
    items:
      - qty: "2 packages (8 ounces each)"
        item: cream cheese
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: mayonnaise
      - qty: "1 packet"
        item: ranch dressing mix
        shop: ranch seasoning mix
  - group: Topping
    items:
      - qty: "1 small bag"
        item: broccoli, cauliflower, and carrot mix (produce section), finely chopped
      - qty: "½"
        item: red onion, finely chopped
      - qty: "1"
        item: green bell pepper, finely chopped
      - qty: "1½ cups"
        item: shredded Colby Jack cheese

steps:
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: Preheat the oven to **350°F**.
  - title: Press in the crust
    text: >-
      Unroll the crescent dough into an **11x17-inch** pan sprayed with cooking
      spray, pressing the seams together to make one sheet.
  - title: Bake and cool
    text: >-
      Bake **12–15 minutes**, until golden brown. Let it cool completely.
  - title: Make the spread
    text: >-
      Blend the cream cheese, mayonnaise, and ranch mix until smooth, then spread
      it over the cooled crust.
  - title: Top
    text: >-
      Scatter the chopped vegetables evenly over the spread. Sprinkle on the
      cheese and press it down gently.
  - title: Chill and cut
    text: >-
      Refrigerate **1–2 hours**, then cut into squares.

notes:
  - Makes about 24 large or 30 small squares. Halve the recipe for a smaller crowd.
  - The source doesn't list times. These are estimates.
  - Soften the cream cheese first so it blends smoothly.
