steps:
  - title: Start the potatoes
    text: >-
      Put the potatoes in a large pot, cover them with cold water by about
      **1 inch**, and add **1 teaspoon** salt. Bring to a boil over **high heat**,
      then turn it down to a simmer and cook **15–20 minutes**, until a fork
      slides in easily. Make the chicken while they cook.
  - title: Flatten the chicken
    text: >-
      If your chicken isn't already thin, place each breast between two sheets
      of plastic wrap and pound it to an even **¼ inch** thick so it cooks quickly
      and evenly.
  - title: Mix the coating
    text: >-
      In a shallow dish, stir together the flour, salt, pepper, and oregano.
  - title: Coat the chicken
    text: >-
      Press each piece of chicken into the flour mixture so both sides are
      covered, then shake off the excess.
  - title: Brown the first side
    text: >-
      Melt **4 tablespoons** butter into the olive oil in a large skillet over **medium heat**.
      Add the chicken and cook until the bottom is lightly browned.
  - title: Add the mushrooms and wine
    text: >-
      Flip the chicken. Scatter the mushrooms around it, then pour in the
      Marsala and the sherry.
  - title: Cover and simmer
    text: >-
      Cover the skillet and simmer for **10 minutes**, turning the chicken once,
      until it's no longer pink in the middle and the juices run clear
      (165°F on a thermometer).
  - title: Mash the potatoes
    text: >-
      While the chicken simmers, drain the potatoes and put them back in the
      pot. Add **4 tablespoons** butter and the warm milk, and mash until smooth.
      Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  - title: Serve
    text: >-
      Spoon the mashed potatoes onto plates, set the chicken on top, and spoon
      the mushrooms and sauce over everything. Serve hot.

notes:
  - For a creamier sauce, take the chicken out after simmering and stir a splash of cream or half-and-half into the pan.
  - Freezes for up to 2 months. Thaw in the fridge and reheat in a 350°F oven.
