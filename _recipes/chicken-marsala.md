---
title: Chicken Marsala
summary: >-
  Thin, pan-fried chicken breasts in a sweet Marsala wine and mushroom sauce.
  Quick enough for a weeknight, nice enough for company.
category: Dinner
# image: /assets/images/recipes/chicken-marsala.jpg
# image_alt: Chicken Marsala with mushrooms in a skillet
time:
  prep: 10
  cook: 20
servings: 4
calories: 448
source:
  name: Allrecipes
  author: Allrecipes Member
  url: https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/8887/chicken-marsala/

ingredients:
  - group: Coating
    items:
      - qty: "¼ cup"
        item: all-purpose flour
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: salt
      - qty: "¼ teaspoon"
        item: ground black pepper
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: dried oregano
  - group: Chicken and sauce
    items:
      - qty: "4 medium"
        item: skinless, boneless chicken breast halves, pounded ¼ inch thick
        shop: boneless skinless chicken breasts
      - qty: "4 tablespoons"
        item: butter
      - qty: "4 tablespoons"
        item: olive oil
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: sliced mushrooms
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: Marsala wine
      - qty: "¼ cup"
        item: cooking sherry

steps:
  - title: Flatten the chicken
    text: >-
      If your chicken isn't already thin, place each breast between two sheets
      of plastic wrap and pound it to an even **¼ inch** thick so it cooks quickly
      and evenly.
  - title: Mix the coating
    text: >-
      In a shallow dish, stir together the flour, salt, pepper, and oregano.
  - title: Coat the chicken
    text: >-
      Press each piece of chicken into the flour mixture so both sides are
      covered, then shake off the excess.
  - title: Brown the first side
    text: >-
      Melt the butter into the olive oil in a large skillet over **medium heat**.
      Add the chicken and cook until the bottom is lightly browned.
  - title: Add the mushrooms and wine
    text: >-
      Flip the chicken. Scatter the mushrooms around it, then pour in the
      Marsala and the sherry.
  - title: Cover and simmer
    text: >-
      Cover the skillet and simmer for **10 minutes**, turning the chicken once,
      until it's no longer pink in the middle and the juices run clear
      (165°F on a thermometer).
  - title: Serve
    text: >-
      Plate the chicken and spoon the mushrooms and sauce over the top. Serve hot.

notes:
  - Good with rice, risotto, mashed potatoes, or crusty bread for the sauce.
  - For a creamier sauce, take the chicken out after simmering and stir a splash of cream or half-and-half into the pan.
  - Freezes for up to 2 months. Thaw in the fridge and reheat in a 350°F oven.
