---
title: Bacon Wrapped Jalapeño Poppers
summary: >-
  Jalapeño halves stuffed with a creamy cheddar filling, wrapped in bacon, and
  baked until crisp. The perfect party appetizer.
category: Appetizer
# image: /assets/images/recipes/bacon-wrapped-jalapeno-poppers.jpg
# image_alt: Bacon-wrapped jalapeño poppers on a sheet pan
time:
  prep: 25
  cook: 25
servings: 12
calories: 198
yield: 24 poppers
source:
  name: Dinner at the Zoo
  author: Sara Welch
  url: https://www.dinneratthezoo.com/bacon-wrapped-jalapeno-poppers/

ingredients:
  - items:
      - qty: "12"
        item: jalapeño peppers, about 3–4 inches long
      - qty: "1 block (8 ounces)"
        item: cream cheese, softened
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: cheddar cheese
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: onion powder
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: salt
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: pepper
      - qty: "12 slices"
        item: bacon (not thick-cut)
      - qty: ""
        item: cooking spray

steps:
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: >-
      Preheat the oven to **400°F**. Line a sheet pan with foil and coat it with
      cooking spray.
  - title: Prep the peppers
    text: >-
      Halve the jalapeños lengthwise and scoop out the seeds and ribs with a
      spoon.
  - title: Mix the filling
    text: >-
      In a medium bowl, stir together the cream cheese, cheddar, onion powder,
      salt, and pepper.
  - title: Fill
    text: Spoon the cheese mixture into each jalapeño half.
  - title: Wrap in bacon
    text: >-
      Cut the bacon slices in half crosswise. Wrap a piece around each pepper
      half and hold it in place with a toothpick.
  - title: Bake
    text: >-
      Arrange on the sheet pan and bake **20–25 minutes**, until the bacon is
      crisp and browned. Serve right away.

notes:
  - Wear gloves while seeding the jalapeños, and don't touch your eyes.
  - Thick-cut bacon won't crisp before the peppers are done.
---
