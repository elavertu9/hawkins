---
title: Jalapeño Popper Deviled Eggs
summary: >-
  Deviled eggs with a kick: a creamy cream cheese filling with jalapeño,
  garlic, and crispy bacon.
category: Appetizer
# image: /assets/images/recipes/jalapeno-popper-deviled-eggs.jpg
# image_alt: Deviled eggs topped with bacon and jalapeño slices
time:
  prep: 20
  cook: 10
servings: 20
yield: 20 deviled eggs
source:
  name: Spicy Southern Kitchen
  author: Christin Mahrlig
  url: https://spicysouthernkitchen.com/jalapeno-popper-deviled-eggs/

ingredients:
  - group: Filling
    items:
      - qty: "6 slices"
        item: bacon, chopped
      - qty: "1"
        item: jalapeño, seeded and chopped
      - qty: "1 clove"
        item: garlic, minced
      - qty: "10"
        item: hard-boiled eggs
      - qty: "3 ounces"
        item: cream cheese, softened
      - qty: "3 tablespoons"
        item: mayonnaise
      - qty: "1 teaspoon"
        item: Dijon mustard
      - qty: "¼ teaspoon"
        item: apple cider vinegar
      - qty: "1 pinch"
        item: salt
      - qty: "1 pinch"
        item: cayenne pepper
  - group: Garnish
    items:
      - qty: "1"
        item: jalapeño, thinly sliced

steps:
  - title: Crisp the bacon
    text: >-
      Cook the bacon in a nonstick skillet until crispy. Lift it out with a
      slotted spoon and set it aside.
  - title: Cook the jalapeño and garlic
    text: >-
      Pour off all but **½ tablespoon** of the bacon grease. Add the chopped
      jalapeño and garlic and cook **1–2 minutes**.
  - title: Make the filling
    text: >-
      Halve the eggs and pop the yolks into a medium bowl. Add the cream cheese,
      mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar, salt, and cayenne, and mash with a fork until
      smooth.
  - title: Add the mix-ins
    text: >-
      Stir in the jalapeño and garlic. Set aside about **a quarter** of the bacon
      and stir the rest into the filling.
  - title: Fill and garnish
    text: >-
      Spoon the filling into the egg whites. Top with the reserved bacon and the
      jalapeño slices.

notes:
  - The source doesn't list times. These are estimates and don't include boiling the eggs.
  - Pickled jalapeños work in place of fresh.
---
