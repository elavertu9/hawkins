---
title: Grasshopper Pie
summary: >-
  Grandma's classic frozen mint pie: a fluffy, minty-green marshmallow filling
  in a chocolate Oreo crust.
category: Dessert
# image: /assets/images/recipes/grasshopper-pie.jpg
# image_alt: Slice of green grasshopper pie with chocolate shavings
time:
  prep: 15
  cook: 30
  additional: 180
servings: 8
calories: 355
yield: 1 (10-inch) pie
source:
  name: Simply Recipes
  author: Elise Bauer
  url: https://www.simplyrecipes.com/recipes/grasshopper_pie/

ingredients:
  - group: Crust
    items:
      - qty: "30"
        item: Oreo cookies (60 halves), filling discarded, crushed fine
      - qty: "6 tablespoons"
        item: unsalted butter, melted
  - group: Filling
    items:
      - qty: "¾ cup"
        item: whole milk
      - qty: "24 large"
        item: marshmallows
      - qty: "¼ cup"
        item: crème de menthe
      - qty: "2 tablespoons"
        item: white crème de cacao
      - qty: "1 to 2 drops"
        item: green food coloring (optional)
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: heavy cream
  - group: Topping
    items:
      - qty: ""
        item: chocolate shavings (optional)

steps:
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: Preheat the oven to **425°F**.
  - title: Make the crust
    text: >-
      Mix the Oreo crumbs with the melted butter. A small handful should hold
      together when you squeeze it. If it crumbles, add water
      **1 tablespoon** at a time until it does.
  - title: Press and bake
    text: >-
      Press the crumbs into the bottom and up the sides of a **10-inch** pie dish,
      packing them down firmly with the bottom of a cup. Bake **5–10 minutes**,
      let cool completely, then move it to the freezer.
  - title: Melt the marshmallows
    text: >-
      In a saucepan over **medium heat**, warm the milk until steaming but not
      boiling. Add the marshmallows and stir gently until melted.
  - title: Cool the mixture
    text: >-
      Take the pan off the heat and let it cool to room temperature, then chill
      until it's cool to the touch. An ice-water bath speeds this up. If it's
      still warm, the filling can break in the next step.
  - title: Add the liqueurs
    text: >-
      Stir the crème de menthe and crème de cacao into the cooled marshmallow
      mixture. Add a drop or two of green food coloring if you like.
  - title: Whip and fold in the cream
    text: >-
      Whip the cream to stiff peaks. Fold it into the marshmallow mixture in
      **3 additions**, folding gently until no white streaks remain.
  - title: Fill and freeze
    text: >-
      Pour the filling into the crust and freeze for at least **3 hours**. Serve
      with a dollop of whipped cream and chocolate shavings.

notes:
  - Instead of splitting Oreos, use 1¼ cups crushed chocolate wafers, or a graham cracker crust.
  - Contains alcohol from the liqueurs.
  - Make ahead and keep it frozen. Cover it loosely with foil after the first 3 hours.
