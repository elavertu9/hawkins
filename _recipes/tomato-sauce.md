---
title: Tomato Sauce
summary: >-
  A slow-simmered, chunky tomato sauce with garlic, red pepper, oregano, basil,
  and mint, finished with Parmesan and butter.
category: Sauce
tags: [Make Ahead, Vegetarian]
added: 2026-10-24
# image: /assets/images/recipes/tomato-sauce.jpg
# image_alt: Pot of chunky red tomato sauce
time:
  prep: 10
  cook: 95
servings: 8
calories: 175
calories_estimated: true
yield: About 6 cups

ingredients:
  - group: Aromatics
    items:
      - qty: "⅓ cup"
        item: good-quality extra virgin olive oil
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: red pepper flakes
      - qty: "4 cloves"
        item: garlic, minced
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: minced onion
        shop: onion
      - qty: "1 teaspoon"
        item: oregano, divided
      - qty: "1 tablespoon"
        item: basil, divided
      - qty: "1 teaspoon"
        item: mint, divided
      - qty: "1 teaspoon"
        item: sugar
      - qty: "1 teaspoon"
        item: salt
      - qty: "¼ teaspoon"
        item: black pepper
  - group: Sauce
    items:
      - qty: "2 cans (28 ounces each)"
        item: whole tomatoes
  - group: To finish
    items:
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
      - qty: "2 tablespoons"
        item: unsalted butter

steps:
  - title: Crush the tomatoes
    text: >-
      Pour both cans of tomatoes into a bowl and crush them with your hand,
      leaving some chunks.
  - title: Bloom the aromatics
    text: >-
      Heat the olive oil in a large heavy-bottomed saucepan over
      **medium-low heat**. Add the red pepper flakes, garlic, onion, sugar, salt,
      pepper, and most of the oregano, basil, and mint, saving a pinch of each for
      the end. Simmer gently about **5 minutes**, until the onion is translucent
      and the oil smells of the herbs.
  - title: Simmer
    text: >-
      Turn the heat to **medium-high** and add the tomatoes. Bring to a boil, then
      turn to **low**, partly cover, and simmer **90 minutes**, stirring a couple
      of times so it doesn't stick.
  - title: Finish
    text: >-
      Take the pot off the heat and stir in the reserved herbs, the Parmesan, and
      the butter.

notes:
  - Family recipe. The original doesn't list times or servings. Those are estimates, with a serving of about ¾ cup.
  - Serve over pasta, or with meatballs. It's the same sauce as in [Italian Meatballs & Sauce](/recipes/italian-meatballs-and-sauce/).
