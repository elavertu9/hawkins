---
title: Easy Goulash
summary: >-
  Classic American goulash: ground beef, macaroni, and tomatoes simmered in one
  pot with Italian seasoning, then finished with melted cheddar.
category: Dinner
added: 2026-10-24
# image: /assets/images/recipes/goulash.jpg
# image_alt: Pot of beef and macaroni goulash with melted cheddar
time:
  prep: 5
  cook: 30
servings: 10
calories: 388
source:
  name: I Wash You Dry
  author: Shawn
  url: https://iwashyoudry.com/easy-goulash-recipe/

ingredients:
  - group: Beef
    items:
      - qty: "1 tablespoon"
        item: olive oil
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: onion, diced
      - qty: "2 pounds"
        item: lean ground beef
      - qty: "3 teaspoons"
        item: minced garlic
  - group: Sauce and pasta
    items:
      - qty: "2 cans (15 ounces each)"
        item: tomato sauce
      - qty: "2 cans (15 ounces each)"
        item: petite diced tomatoes
      - qty: "3 cups"
        item: beef broth
      - qty: "3 tablespoons"
        item: Worcestershire sauce
      - qty: "2 teaspoons"
        item: seasoned salt
      - qty: "2 tablespoons"
        item: Italian seasoning
      - qty: "3"
        item: bay leaves
      - qty: "2 cups"
        item: uncooked macaroni
  - group: To finish
    items:
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: shredded cheddar cheese

steps:
  - title: Brown the beef
    text: >-
      Heat the oil in a large pot over **medium-high heat**. Add the onion and
      beef and cook until the beef is no longer pink. Drain off the fat and
      return the pot to the stove.
  - title: Add the garlic
    text: Stir in the garlic and cook until fragrant, about **30 seconds**.
  - title: Add everything else
    text: >-
      Pour in the tomato sauce, diced tomatoes, broth, and Worcestershire. Add the
      seasoned salt, Italian seasoning, bay leaves, and the dry macaroni. Stir and
      bring to a boil.
  - title: Simmer the pasta
    text: >-
      Lower the heat to a gentle boil and cook, stirring now and then, until the
      macaroni is tender, about **20 minutes**.
  - title: Finish with cheese
    text: >-
      Pull out the bay leaves and stir in the cheddar just before serving.

notes:
  - The source's steps also mention a bell pepper, but it isn't in its ingredient list. Add 1 diced bell pepper with the onion if you like.
