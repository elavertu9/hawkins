ingredients:
  - group: Soup
    items:
      - qty: "1 pound"
        item: Italian sausage (mild works well)
      - qty: "1 large"
        item: Vidalia onion, chopped
        shop: sweet onion
      - qty: "2 cloves"
        item: garlic, minced (or 1 tablespoon jarred minced garlic)
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: salt
      - qty: "¼ teaspoon"
        item: black pepper
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: red pepper flakes
      - qty: "1 teaspoon"
        item: Accent seasoning (optional)
      - qty: "2 large"
        item: russet potatoes, washed, halved, and cut into ¼-inch slices
      - qty: "2 cans (8 ounces each)"
        item: chicken broth (add 1 envelope chicken bouillon for extra flavor)
      - qty: "1 quart"
        item: water
      - qty: "2 teaspoons"
        item: flour or cornstarch, mixed with enough water to make a paste
  - group: To finish
    items:
      - qty: "2 cups"
        item: kale or Swiss chard, chopped (packed)
      - qty: "4 slices"
        item: cooked bacon, chopped
        shop: bacon
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: heavy whipping cream, tempered

steps:
  - title: Brown the sausage
    text: >-
      Cut the sausage into small pieces. Brown it in your soup pot over
      **medium-high heat**, breaking it up as it cooks.
  - title: Add the aromatics
    text: >-
      Add the onion, garlic, salt, pepper, red pepper flakes, and Accent if using.
      Cook until the onion turns translucent.
  - title: Add the potatoes and liquid
    text: >-
      Turn the heat down to **medium**. Add the potatoes, then slowly pour in the
      broth and water and stir.
  - title: Simmer until tender
    text: >-
      Cook on **medium**, stirring occasionally, until the potatoes are tender.
  - title: Thicken
    text: >-
      Stir in the flour or cornstarch paste and simmer **15 minutes** more.
  - title: Finish with kale and cream
    text: >-
      Turn the heat to its **lowest setting**. Sprinkle in the kale and bacon,
      then slowly pour in the cream while stirring.
  - title: Serve
    text: Taste, adjust the seasoning, and serve.

notes:
  - To temper the cream, whisk a ladle of hot broth into it before adding it to the pot, so it doesn't curdle.
  - If your broth tastes weak, add a chicken bouillon envelope.
  - The source lists 1 hour total. The prep and cook split is an estimate.
  - The calorie estimate assumes 6 servings.
