---
title: Tzatziki Sauce
summary: >-
  Cool, creamy Greek yogurt sauce with grated cucumber, garlic, olive oil, and
  fresh dill. Even better after a night in the fridge.
category: Sauce
# image: /assets/images/recipes/tzatziki-sauce.jpg
# image_alt: Bowl of tzatziki topped with dill and olive oil
time:
  prep: 15
  cook: 0
servings: 8
calories: 80
calories_estimated: true
yield: About 2 cups

ingredients:
  - items:
      - qty: "1 medium"
        item: cucumber
      - qty: "1½ cups"
        item: plain full-fat Greek yogurt
      - qty: "2 cloves"
        item: garlic, minced
      - qty: "2 tablespoons"
        item: extra virgin olive oil
      - qty: "1 tablespoon"
        item: white vinegar
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: salt
      - qty: "1 tablespoon"
        item: fresh dill, minced

steps:
  - title: Grate and drain the cucumber
    text: >-
      Grate the cucumber, then squeeze it in cheesecloth over a bowl to drain the
      juice. Discard the juice and keep the cucumber.
  - title: Mix
    text: >-
      Stir together the yogurt, garlic, olive oil, vinegar, salt, dill, and grated
      cucumber until well combined.
  - title: Rest (optional)
    text: >-
      For the best flavor, cover and refrigerate for **24 hours** before serving.

notes:
  - Family recipe. The original doesn't list times or servings. Those are estimates, with a serving of ¼ cup.
  - Serve with pita bread, vegetables, or [Lamb & Feta Meatballs](/recipes/lamb-and-feta-meatballs/).
