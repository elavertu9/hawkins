---
title: Fruit Pizza
summary: >-
  A sugar cookie crust spread with sweet vanilla cream cheese, topped with
  kiwi, strawberries, and blueberries, and glazed with apple jelly.
category: Dessert
# image: /assets/images/recipes/fruit-pizza.jpg
# image_alt: Round fruit pizza topped with kiwi, strawberries, and blueberries
time:
  prep: 20
  cook: 20
  additional: 90
servings: 12
calories: 320
yield: 1 (12-inch) pizza
source:
  name: Pillsbury
  author: Pillsbury Kitchens
  url: https://www.pillsbury.com/recipes/easy-fruit-pizza/a765c571-37d5-4916-8747-e944a923c83b

ingredients:
  - group: Crust
    items:
      - qty: "1 roll (16.5 ounces)"
        item: refrigerated sugar cookie dough
  - group: Filling
    items:
      - qty: "1 package (8 ounces)"
        item: cream cheese, softened
      - qty: "⅓ cup"
        item: sugar
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: vanilla
  - group: Topping
    items:
      - qty: "2"
        item: kiwifruit, peeled, halved lengthwise, and sliced
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: halved or quartered fresh strawberries (6 to 8 medium)
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: fresh or frozen blueberries
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: apple jelly

steps:
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: >-
      Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Spray a **12-inch** pizza pan with cooking
      spray.
  - title: Press in the crust
    text: >-
      Break up the cookie dough into the pan and press it out evenly, leaving
      **½ inch** bare around the edge.
  - title: Bake and cool
    text: >-
      Bake **16–20 minutes**, until golden brown. Cool completely on a rack,
      about **30 minutes**.
  - title: Make the filling
    text: >-
      Beat the cream cheese, sugar, and vanilla on **medium** for about
      **30 seconds**, until fluffy. Spread it over the cooled crust.
  - title: Add the fruit
    text: >-
      Arrange the fruit over the filling. Stir the jelly until smooth, then brush
      or spoon it over the fruit.
  - title: Chill and serve
    text: >-
      Refrigerate at least **1 hour**. Cut into wedges, or 4 rows by 3 for
      squares. Keep leftovers covered in the fridge.

notes:
  - Line the pan with parchment cut 2 inches bigger for easy cleanup. A dab of butter under it holds it in place. Don't spray the parchment.
  - Stir 1 teaspoon grated lemon zest into the filling for a bright, tart edge.
  - No pizza pan? Press the dough into a 12-inch circle on a large cookie sheet.
  - Pat the fruit dry on paper towels so the juices don't bleed into the filling.
  - Press the dough while it's cold from the fridge. It sticks less.
---
