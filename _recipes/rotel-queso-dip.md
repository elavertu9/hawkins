title: RO*TEL Famous Queso Dip
summary: >-
  The classic two-ingredient party dip: melted Velveeta and RO*TEL tomatoes
  with green chilies. Creamy, cheesy, a little spicy, and done in 10 minutes.
category: Appetizer
# image: /assets/images/recipes/rotel-queso-dip.jpg
# image_alt: Bowl of queso dip with tortilla chips
time:
  prep: 5
  cook: 5
servings: 20
calories: 68
source:
  name: RO*TEL
  author: RO*TEL Kitchens
  url: https://www.ro-tel.com/recipes/sides-appetizers/rotel-famous-queso-dip-2693

ingredients:
  - items:
      - qty: "1 can (10 ounces)"
        item: RO*TEL Original Diced Tomatoes & Green Chilies, undrained
      - qty: "1 package (16 ounces)"
        item: Velveeta Original, cut into ½-inch cubes
        shop: Velveeta
  - group: For serving
    items:
      - qty: ""
        item: tortilla chips, crackers, or cut-up fresh vegetables
        shop: tortilla chips

steps:
  - title: Combine
    text: >-
      Put the undrained tomatoes and the Velveeta cubes in a medium saucepan.
  - title: Melt
    text: >-
      Cook over **medium heat** for about **5 minutes**, stirring often, until
      the Velveeta is completely melted and the dip is smooth.
  - title: Serve
    text: Serve warm with tortilla chips, crackers, or fresh vegetables.

notes:
  - "Make it better: stir in some browned, drained hamburg."
  - "Microwave: combine the tomatoes and Velveeta in a covered 1½-quart microwave-safe dish. Microwave on HIGH for 5 minutes, stirring after 3, until melted, then stir until smooth. The dish gets hot."
  - For a double batch, microwave on HIGH for 8 minutes, stirring after 5.
  - For a thicker dip, drain the tomatoes.
