---
title: Baked Ziti
summary: >-
  A big, crowd-sized baked ziti layered with a beef and sausage meat sauce,
  provolone, mozzarella, and a creamy sour cream and cottage cheese middle.
category: Dinner

time:
  prep: 30
  cook: 60

  name: Allrecipes

ingredients:
  - group: Pasta
    items:
      - qty: "1 pound"
        item: dry ziti pasta
  - group: Meat sauce
    items:
      - qty: "1 ½ tablespoons"
        item: olive oil
      - qty: "1"
        item: onion, sliced
      - qty: "1 teaspoon"
        item: minced fresh rosemary
      - qty: "4 cloves"
        item: garlic, chopped
      - qty: "½ pound"
        item: ground beef
      - qty: "½ pound"
        item: ground pork sausage
      - qty: "1 ½"
        item: (26-ounce) jars spaghetti sauce
      - qty: ""
        item: salt, to taste
  - group: Cheese layers
    items:
      - qty: "6 ounces"
        item: provolone cheese, sliced
      - qty: "¾ cup"
        item: sour cream
      - qty: "¾ cup"
        item: cottage cheese
      - qty: "6 ounces"
        item: mozzarella cheese, shredded
      - qty: "2 tablespoons"
        item: freshly grated Parmesan cheese

steps:
  - title: Cook the pasta
    text: >-
      Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook the ziti until
      al dente, **8 to 10 minutes**, then drain.
  - title: Cook the onion, garlic, and rosemary
    text: >-
      Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a large, heavy skillet over **medium heat**.
      Cook the onion until tender, then stir in the rosemary and garlic. Scoop it
      all into a small bowl.
  - title: Brown the meat
    text: >-
      Add the ground beef and sausage to the same skillet. Cook over
      **medium-high heat**, breaking it up, until evenly browned.
  - title: Simmer the sauce
    text: >-
      Stir the onion mixture back in along with the spaghetti sauce, and season
      with salt. Turn the heat to low and simmer for **10 minutes**.
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: >-
      While the sauce simmers, preheat the oven to **350°F (175°C)** and grease a
      9x13-inch baking dish.
  - title: Build the first layer
    text: >-
      Spread **half** the pasta in the dish. Top it with the provolone slices, then
      the sour cream and cottage cheese, then a little **less than half** of the
      meat sauce.
  - title: Build the second layer
    text: >-
      Add the rest of the pasta, then the mozzarella, the remaining meat sauce,
      and a sprinkle of Parmesan.
  - title: Bake
    text: >-
      Bake until heated through and the cheese is melted, **20 to 30 minutes**.

notes:
  - Stir the sour cream and cottage cheese together before layering so they spread evenly.
  - Feeds a crowd. Halve everything and use an 8x8-inch dish for a smaller batch.
  - Assemble ahead, cover, and refrigerate. Bake a little longer if it goes into the oven cold.
