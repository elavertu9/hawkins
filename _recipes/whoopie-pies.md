steps:
  - title: Cream the sugar
    text: Cream the sugar, shortening, and eggs together.
  - title: Add the dry ingredients
    text: >-
      Sift the flour, baking soda, baking powder, salt, and cocoa, then blend them
      into the creamed mixture.
  - title: Finish the batter
    text: >-
      Mix in the sour milk, vanilla, and boiling water until smooth.
  - title: Bake
    text: >-
      Drop the batter by the teaspoon onto ungreased cookie sheets. Bake at
      **450°F** for **10–12 minutes**. Let them cool completely.
  - title: Cook the filling paste
    text: >-
      Whisk the flour and milk in a small saucepan and cook, stirring, until it's
      as thick as pudding. Let it cool completely.
  - title: Make the cream
    text: >-
      Cream the confectioners' sugar, salt, vanilla, margarine or butter, and
      shortening.
  - title: Combine the filling
    text: >-
      Beat the cooled paste into the cream **1 teaspoon** at a time, beating well
      after each addition, until light and fluffy.
  - title: Assemble
    text: >-
      Spread filling on the flat side of one cake and top with a second.

notes:
  - Family recipe, from a handwritten card titled "Gobs." It doesn't list servings, times, or yield. Those are estimates.
  - The card says 450°F. That's hot, so check the first batch early.
  - "No sour milk? Stir 1 tablespoon vinegar or lemon juice into 1 cup milk and let it sit 5 minutes."
  - The paste must be completely cool before you beat it in, or the filling will melt.
  - Wrap each whoopie pie in plastic wrap to keep them soft.
