steps:
  - title: Shape the shells
    text: >-
      Preheat the oven to **375°F**. Spray or brush the wonton wrappers with a
      little oil, then drape them over the edges of a **9×13** baking dish.
  - title: Bake the shells
    text: >-
      Bake for **7–8 minutes**, watching closely so they don't burn. While they're
      still pliable, gently pull them apart so they're easier to fill. Bake
      another **7–8 minutes**, until crisp.
  - title: Cook the chicken
    text: >-
      Toss the raw chicken with the hoisin, sesame oil, soy sauce, garlic, and
      ginger. Cook in a large skillet over **high heat** for **7–8 minutes**,
      tossing often, until cooked through.
  - title: Make the slaw
    text: >-
      While the chicken cooks, mix the coleslaw, green onions, sesame oil, rice
      vinegar, soy sauce, and honey in a large bowl.
  - title: Fill and serve
    text: >-
      Spoon the chicken into the shells and top with slaw. Drizzle with sweet
      chili sauce and sprinkle with cilantro and sesame seeds.

notes:
  - For low-carb, use lettuce wraps instead of wonton shells.
  - Leftover chicken keeps 3–4 days in the fridge or up to 3 months in the freezer.
