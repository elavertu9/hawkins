---
title: Almond Raspberry Cookies
summary: >-
  Thumbprint cookies from store-bought sugar cookie dough, with chopped almonds,
  a dab of raspberry jam, and a dusting of powdered sugar.
category: Dessert
# image: /assets/images/recipes/almond-raspberry-cookies.jpg
# image_alt: Almond thumbprint cookies filled with raspberry jam
time:
  prep: 15
  cook: 35
servings: 48
calories: 80
calories_estimated: true
yield: About 4 dozen cookies
source:
  name: Pampered Chef
  author: Pampered Chef
  url: https://www.pamperedchef.ca/recipe/Desserts/Almond+Raspberry+Cookies/1039456

ingredients:
  - items:
      - qty: "1 package (20 oz/575 g)"
        item: refrigerated sugar cookie dough
      - qty: "1 cup (250 mL)"
        item: blanched slivered almonds, chopped
      - qty: "1 teaspoon (5 mL)"
        item: almond extract
      - qty: "½ cup (125 mL)"
        item: red raspberry preserves
      - qty: "⅔ cup (150 mL)"
        item: powdered sugar

steps:
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: Preheat the oven to **350°F (176°C)**.
  - title: Mix the dough
    text: >-
      Chop the almonds very finely. Put the cookie dough in a large bowl, add the
      almonds and almond extract, and mix well.
  - title: Scoop and press
    text: >-
      Use a small cookie scoop to drop the dough **2 inches** apart on a baking
      stone or baking sheet. Press a lightly floured finger into the center of
      each to make a well.
  - title: Add the jam
    text: >-
      Fill each well with about **½ teaspoon** of raspberry preserves.
  - title: Bake
    text: >-
      Bake for **15–18 minutes**, until light golden brown. Move the cookies to a
      cooling rack set over parchment paper.
  - title: Dust with sugar
    text: Once they're cool, sprinkle them with powdered sugar.

notes:
  - The source doesn't list prep or cook times. These are estimates, with cook time covering two batches.
  - Any jam works. Apricot and strawberry are good swaps.
---
