---
title: Corn Soufflé
summary: >-
  The easy holiday corn casserole: corn muffin mix, two kinds of canned corn,
  butter, and sour cream baked until set, golden, and spoonable.
category: Side
# image: /assets/images/recipes/corn-souffle.jpg
# image_alt: Golden corn casserole in a baking dish
time:
  prep: 15
  cook: 45
servings: 8
calories: 256
source:
  name: the hungry bluebird
  author: Kelly Wildenhaus
  url: https://thehungrybluebird.com/corn-souffle/

ingredients:
  - items:
      - qty: "1 can (15 ounces)"
        item: whole kernel corn, drained
      - qty: "1 can (15 ounces)"
        item: cream-style corn
      - qty: "1"
        item: egg, lightly beaten
      - qty: "4 tablespoons"
        item: butter, melted
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: sour cream
      - qty: "1 box (8½ ounces)"
        item: corn muffin mix (such as Jiffy)

steps:
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: >-
      Preheat the oven to **350°F** and lightly butter a **2-quart** casserole
      dish.
  - title: Mix
    text: Stir all the ingredients together until well combined.
  - title: Bake
    text: >-
      Pour into the dish and bake **45–55 minutes**, until set and firm to the
      touch but not dry. A deeper dish takes longer.

notes:
  - Start checking at 45 minutes. It should be set, not jiggly.
  - The source has only tested Jiffy. If you use another brand, use the same amount.
  - Leftovers reheat well in the microwave. Cover loosely with a damp paper towel.
