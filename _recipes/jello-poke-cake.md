---
title: Jello Poke Cake
summary: >-
  A boxed white cake poked full of holes, soaked with strawberry Jell-O, chilled,
  and topped with whipped topping. Cool, creamy, and bright pink inside.
category: Dessert
tags: [Make Ahead, Crowd]
added: 2026-10-24
# image: /assets/images/recipes/jello-poke-cake.jpg
# image_alt: Slice of white cake streaked with red Jell-O and topped with whipped cream
time:
  prep: 10
  cook: 25
  additional: 195
servings: 15
calories: 205
yield: 1 (9x13-inch) cake
source:
  name: Lil' Luna
  author: Kristyn Merkley
  url: https://lilluna.com/jello-poke-cake/

ingredients:
  - group: Cake
    items:
      - qty: "1 box (15.25 ounces)"
        item: white cake mix, plus the ingredients listed on the box
  - group: Jell-O
    items:
      - qty: "1 box (3 ounces)"
        item: strawberry Jell-O (or raspberry or cherry)
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: boiling water
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: cold water
  - group: Topping
    items:
      - qty: "1 tub (8 ounces)"
        item: whipped topping (or 2 cups whipped cream)
        shop: frozen whipped topping

steps:
  - title: Bake the cake
    text: >-
      Make the cake as the box directs and bake it in a **9x13-inch** dish. Let it
      cool for **15 minutes**.
  - title: Poke holes
    text: >-
      Poke the cake all over with a fork, every **½ to 1 inch**.
  - title: Make the Jell-O
    text: >-
      Stir the Jell-O powder into the **1 cup** boiling water until dissolved,
      then stir in the **1 cup** cold water.
  - title: Soak the cake
    text: >-
      Spoon the Jell-O over the cake a little at a time, letting it soak in, until
      it's all absorbed.
  - title: Chill and top
    text: >-
      Refrigerate for **3–4 hours**, until set. Spread on the whipped topping just
      before serving.

notes:
  - Use only 1 cup boiling and 1 cup cold water, not the amounts on the Jell-O box, or the cake gets soggy.
  - White cake works with any Jell-O flavor. A from-scratch or plain store-bought white cake works too.
  - "Cupcakes: bake in foil liners, poke the cooled cupcakes with a fork or skewer, drizzle in the Jell-O with a spoon, and chill."
  - Keep it covered in the fridge for 2–3 days, or wrap in foil and freeze for up to 3 months.
