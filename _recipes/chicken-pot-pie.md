---
title: Chicken Pot Pie
summary: >-
  A from-scratch chicken pot pie with carrots, peas, and celery in a creamy,
  lightly seasoned sauce, baked between two flaky crusts.
category: Dinner
# image: /assets/images/recipes/chicken-pot-pie.jpg
# image_alt: Golden chicken pot pie with a slice cut out
time:
  prep: 20
  cook: 50
  additional: 10
servings: 8
yield: 1 (9-inch) pie
source:
  name: Allrecipes
  author: Robbie Rice
  url: https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/26317/chicken-pot-pie-ix/

ingredients:
  - group: Filling
    items:
      - qty: "1 pound"
        item: skinless, boneless chicken breasts, cubed
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: sliced carrots
      - qty: "1 cup"
        item: frozen green peas
      - qty: "½ cup"
        item: sliced celery
  - group: Sauce
    items:
      - qty: "⅓ cup"
        item: butter
      - qty: "⅓ cup"
        item: chopped onion
      - qty: "⅓ cup"
        item: all-purpose flour
      - qty: "½ teaspoon"
        item: salt
      - qty: "¼ teaspoon"
        item: black pepper
      - qty: "¼ teaspoon"
        item: celery seeds
      - qty: "1 ¾ cups"
        item: chicken broth
      - qty: "⅔ cup"
        item: milk
  - group: Crust
    items:
      - qty: "2"
        item: (9-inch) unbaked pie crusts

steps:
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: >-
      Preheat the oven to **425°F (220°C)**.
  - title: Boil the chicken and vegetables
    text: >-
      Put the chicken, carrots, peas, and celery in a saucepan and add water to
      cover. Bring to a boil and cook for **15 minutes**, then drain and set aside.
  - title: Soften the onion
    text: >-
      Meanwhile, melt the butter in a second saucepan over **medium heat**. Add the
      onion and cook until soft and translucent, **5 to 7 minutes**.
  - title: Make the roux
    text: >-
      Stir in the flour, salt, pepper, and celery seeds until no dry flour remains.
  - title: Add the broth and milk
    text: >-
      Pour in the chicken broth and milk slowly, stirring the whole time so the
      sauce stays smooth.
  - title: Thicken the sauce
    text: >-
      Turn the heat to **medium-low** and simmer until thick, **5 to 10 minutes**.
      Take it off the heat.
  - title: Fill the pie
    text: >-
      Fit one crust into a 9-inch pie plate. Add the chicken and vegetables, then
      pour the hot sauce over the top.
  - title: Add the top crust
    text: >-
      Lay the second crust over the filling. Seal the edges, trim off the excess,
      and cut a few small slits in the top so steam can escape.
  - title: Bake
    text: >-
      Bake until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbling,
      **30 to 35 minutes**.
  - title: Rest, then serve
    text: >-
      Let the pie cool for **10 minutes** before cutting so the filling holds together.

notes:
  - Set a baking sheet on the rack below the pie to catch drips.
  - "Shortcut: skip boiling the chicken and use cubed rotisserie chicken. Boil just the vegetables until tender."
  - For a saucier pie, use a deep-dish plate and double the sauce.
  - Keeps wrapped in foil in the fridge for 3 to 5 days. Reheat in the oven to keep the crust crisp.
